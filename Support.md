If you've encountered a bug or crash, please include the following details in your email so we can help you faster:

- Device model (e.g., iPhone 17 Pro, iPad Pro, MacBook, Apple Vision Pro)
- OS version (e.g., iOS 26.0, macOS 26.0, visionOS 26.0)
- The FlexVision version (**Settings → About → Version**)
- A description of what happened and what you were doing at the time

You can also report issues on our [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/Meshy-SC/FlexVision/issues).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I play?**
A: Every level shows you a target figure and, below it, a set of connected strokes that *almost* forms it. Drag the end of a stroke onto a different dot on the grid to reshape it. Keep going until your drawing matches the target, and the level solves itself the moment it does. Levels are built on 5×5 and 7×7 grids, and some allow more than one stroke to meet at the same dot.

**Q: What counts as a move?**
A: Each time you place a stroke end onto a dot, that's one move. The toolbar under the board holds Undo and Restart, so you can step back a single move or start the level over cleanly at any time.

**Q: How are stars awarded?**
A: Every puzzle has a par — the number of stroke ends you need to place to fold the starting figure back into the target. Solve it in par or fewer for three stars, within 1.2× par for two stars, and anything beyond that still earns one star for finishing. Your best result per level is what's kept, so replaying a level can only improve your rating.

**Q: How is my score calculated?**
A: A level is worth 10 points solved in three stars, 7 in two stars, and 5 in one. Only your best result per level counts, so replaying a level you've already solved adds points only if you beat your previous rating. Three-starring all 1,000 levels is a perfect 10,000.

**Q: How do the worlds work?**
A: FlexVision has 10 worlds — from Iron to Legend — each with 100 puzzle levels. The Iron world is completely free, all 100 levels. For every other world, level 1 is free to try, and the remaining 99 levels are unlocked with a one-time purchase — either that single world, or every paid world at once with the all-worlds bundle.

**Q: Do I have to finish one world before starting another?**
A: No. Progression runs *within* a world: level 1 of every world is always open, and each later level unlocks when you solve the one before it in that same world. Buying a world makes it immediately playable regardless of how far you've come elsewhere.

**Q: What are solution credits?**
A: A solution credit reveals the answer to a level you're stuck on — the board draws the finished figure for you, stroke by stroke, and you can pause and replay it. Spending a credit unlocks that level's solution permanently, so you can watch it again any time at no further cost. You start with one credit, and more can be purchased in packs of 1, 5, or 10.

**Q: How do I sync my progress across devices?**
A: FlexVision uses iCloud to sync your progress automatically. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on all your devices and that iCloud Drive is enabled for FlexVision. Completions, star ratings, best scores, solution credits, and unlocked solutions all follow you between iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

**Q: I lost my progress. Can I recover it?**
A: Your progress is stored in iCloud. If you reinstall the app on a device signed in to the same Apple Account, your progress should restore automatically. Sync can take a moment after a fresh install — if it doesn't appear, please contact us at the email above.

**Q: I bought a world but it's still locked. What do I do?**
A: Open **Settings → Restore Purchases**, or the **Restore Purchases** button on any purchase screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any device signed in to it, at no extra cost.

**Q: Does resetting my progress remove my purchases?**
A: No. **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears level completions, star ratings, and scores only. Purchased worlds, solution credits, and previously unlocked solutions are never revoked.

**Q: Purchases are greyed out or the store says they're turned off. Why?**
A: The device is set to block in-app purchases — usually through Screen Time. On the device, open **Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases** and check that In-App Purchases is set to Allow. If you use Ask to Buy, a purchase instead waits for the family organizer's approval, and the world or credits arrive on their own once it's approved. Level 1 of the world stays free to play while you wait.

**Q: The music or sound effects aren't playing.**
A: Check **Settings → Audio**, where music and sound effects have separate switches. Also make sure your device isn't in silent mode and that another app isn't holding the audio session.

**Q: Can I change how the app looks?**
A: Yes — **Settings → Appearance** offers System, Light, and Dark. System follows your device setting.

**Q: Does FlexVision work offline?**
A: Yes. Every puzzle is built on your device the moment the app launches, so nothing needs downloading and you can play with no network connection. An internet connection is only needed to sync progress via iCloud and to make or restore purchases.

**Q: Is FlexVision accessible?**
A: The app is built with SwiftUI and follows the system text sizes, so it responds to Dynamic Type. Menus, world and level cells, and the in-game toolbar carry VoiceOver labels and hints, and the celebration and solution animations respect Reduce Motion. If there's an accessibility improvement that would help you play, please write to us — we want to hear about it.

**Q: Is my data shared with third parties?**
A: No. FlexVision does not collect personal information or share any data with third parties. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Meshy-SC/FlexVision/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
