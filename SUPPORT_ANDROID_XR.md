# FlexVision Support — Android XR

FlexVision is a line-drawing puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

FlexVision opens as a window in your space. Look and pinch (or point with your hand or a controller) to press, and pinch and move to drag.

- **The goal:** reshape your line until it matches the small target picture on the left.
- **Add a corner:** look at the middle of a line, pinch and drag. Release on a grid dot and a new corner appears there. Release anywhere else and the line springs back.
- **Progress:** the percentage under the target shows how much of it your line covers.
- **Undo** takes back your last corner, **Restart** starts the level over. There is no timer and no move limit.
- **Stars:** three stars for solving a level in the fewest possible moves (par), two within 20 % of par, one otherwise.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb draws the solution for you, corner by corner. It uses one solution credit the first time you watch a level's solution; after that it's free for that level. Watching never counts as solving: the board goes back to the start for you to finish.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, each level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, FlexVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in FlexVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install FlexVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
